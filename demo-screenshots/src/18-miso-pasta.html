Silky miso pasta

20 minutes · serves 2 · cozy

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp white miso · 1 tbsp butter
  • Garlic, chili flakes, lemon
Little trick: Save a mug of pasta water before draining. It makes the sauce glossy.

Finish

Whisk miso with butter and pasta water. Toss with noodles, lemon zest, and a pinch of chili.
